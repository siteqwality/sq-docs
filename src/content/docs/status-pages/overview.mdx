A SiteQwality status page is the public face of your reliability story:

- **Branding** — custom logo, colors, and a custom domain.
- **Components** — the user-visible building blocks of your service ("API," "Dashboard," "Webhooks").
- **Incidents** — publish ongoing and historical incidents with status updates.
- **Subscribers** — let users subscribe to email or webhook updates.
