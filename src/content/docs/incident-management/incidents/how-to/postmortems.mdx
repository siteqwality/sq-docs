Once an incident is over, its **Post-incident** tab holds one **postmortem** and any number of **action items**.

Postmortems and action items are internal. They never appear on a status page.

## Start a postmortem

Open the incident, choose the **Post-incident** tab and click **Start postmortem**. Site Qwality drafts it from the incident, so you start with the facts and fill in the analysis:

- **Impact**: severity, service, when it started, how long it took to acknowledge and resolve, and the affected monitors.
- **Timeline (UTC)**: status and severity changes, acknowledgments, escalations, monitor changes and updates, with who did each.
- Empty sections for the root cause, contributing factors, what went well, what could be better and the action items.

In the console, the person who starts it becomes its owner. The body is Markdown; edit it in the tab and click **Save**.

From the API, `POST /incident/{incident_id}/postmortem` starts it. Send no `body` to get the drafted template, or your own `body` to start from that instead:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.siteqwality.com/incident/$INCIDENT_ID/postmortem \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SITEQWALITY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "owner_user_id": "<user-id>" }'
```

An incident has at most one postmortem.

## Review and publish

A postmortem moves through three statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Being written. Every postmortem starts here. |
| `in_review` | Ready for the team to read and comment on. |
| `published` | Final. `published_at` records when. |

Change the status in the tab, or with `PUT /incident/{incident_id}/postmortem`, which also edits `body` and `owner_user_id` (`null` clears the owner). A postmortem can only be published once the incident is resolved.

Deleting a postmortem (`DELETE /incident/{incident_id}/postmortem`) keeps the incident's action items.

## Add action items

Each action item is one piece of follow-up work: a required title, plus an optional owner (a user in your account), due date and link to the ticket that tracks it. Add them in the tab with **+ Add action item**, or from the API:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.siteqwality.com/incident/$INCIDENT_ID/action_items \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SITEQWALITY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "title": "Add a retry to the payments client", "owner_user_id": "<user-id>", "due_date": "2026-11-03", "external_url": "https://github.com/acme/payments/issues/412" }'
```

An item's `status` is `open`, `done` or `wont_do`. Change it, or any other field, with `PUT /incident/{incident_id}/action_items/{action_item_id}`; send `null` to clear the owner, due date or link.

## Overdue items and the reminder email

An open item is **overdue** from the day after its due date, counted in your organization's timezone (**Timezone** under **Settings → General**; UTC when none is set). Overdue items are flagged in red in the tab and on the Follow-ups list.

At 09:00 (or a few minutes after) in your organization's timezone on the day after the due date, the item's owner gets one email naming the item and the incident, with a link back to its Post-incident tab. Each item sends it once:

- Changing the due date or the owner re-arms it for the new date or person.
- Closing the item before then means no email.
- Items with no owner or no due date never send one, and neither do owners who have left the account.

## The follow-up list

The **Follow-ups** tab on the Incidents page lists every incident's action items, soonest due first, with undated items last. Filter it to **Open**, **Overdue**, **Done**, **Won't do** or **All**, tick **Only mine** to see your own, and change an item's status right in the list. Each row links to its incident's Post-incident tab.

From the API, `GET /incident/action_items` returns the same list. Its filters are in the [reference](/incident-management/incidents/reference/#action-items).

## Merged incidents

A [merged](/incident-management/incidents/how-to/merge-incidents/) incident takes no new postmortem or action item: write them on the incident it was merged into. Its existing postmortem and action items stay where they are and can still be edited and closed.

## Who can do what

Everyone in the account can read postmortems and action items. Viewers and auditors cannot change them. API keys and API clients need `read:incidents` to read and `write:incidents` to change them.

The [MCP server](/integrations/mcp/) offers the same work as tools: `get_incident_postmortem`, `start_incident_postmortem`, `update_incident_postmortem`, `add_action_item`, `update_action_item` and `list_follow_ups`.
