An incident's **participants** are the people working it, each with a role. Participants are users already in your account.

## Roles

| Role | Use for |
|---|---|
| `lead` | The person running the response. An incident has at most one. |
| `responder` | Anyone working the fix. The default. |
| `comms` | The person handling updates and communication. |

The first person to acknowledge an incident that has no lead becomes its `lead` automatically. Making someone else lead demotes the previous lead to `responder`.

## Add participants

In the dashboard, open the incident and click **Add participant** under **Participants**. Choose **Person**, **On-call schedule** or **Escalation policy**, pick who to add and their role, leave **Page them** checked to page them now, and click **Add**.

Through the API, send exactly one of `user_id`, `schedule_id` or `escalation_policy_id`:

| You send | Who is added |
|---|---|
| `user_id` | That user. |
| `schedule_id` | Whoever is on call in that [schedule](/incident-management/on-call/overview/) right now. |
| `escalation_policy_id` | The users and the on-call people at the [policy](/incident-management/escalation/overview/)'s first level. |

Optional fields are `role` (default `responder`) and `page` (default `true`). A lead is one person, so `"role": "lead"` needs `user_id`. Someone already on the incident keeps their role, and is paged again when `page` is `true`. A schedule or policy with nobody on call adds no one and the request fails.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.siteqwality.com/incident/$INCIDENT_ID/participants \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SITEQWALITY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "schedule_id": "<schedule-id>", "role": "responder", "page": true }'
```

## Paging when you add

With `page` left at `true`, each added user is paged through their own [notification rules](/incident-management/notifications/how-to/notification-rules/). Paging needs the incident to belong to a service and not be resolved. Send `"page": false` to record someone who is already on the call without paging them.

## Change a role or remove someone

In the dashboard, pick a new role in the person's row under **Participants**, or open the row's actions and choose **Remove**. Through the API:

```bash
curl -X PUT https://api.siteqwality.com/incident/$INCIDENT_ID/participants/$USER_ID \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SITEQWALITY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "role": "lead" }'
```

`DELETE /incident/{incident_id}/participants/{user_id}` removes the person.
