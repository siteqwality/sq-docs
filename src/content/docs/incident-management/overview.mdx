SiteQwality's incident management features:

- **Incidents** — track active issues with status, severity, and a chronological log.
- **On-call schedules** — rotate responders across timezones with overrides.
- **Escalation policies** — chain notification steps with timeouts and fallbacks.
- **Notification rules & channels** — control who gets paged, how, and when.
- **Maintenance windows** — silence checks during planned work.
