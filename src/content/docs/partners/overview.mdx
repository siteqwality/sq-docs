The partner program is for agencies, MSPs and consultancies who monitor other people's websites. You hold one Site Qwality account, sign one agreement, and get one invoice. Each client you take on gets a **client account**: a separate account with its own monitors, status page, alert routing and history, which you manage from inside your own login.

There is no self-serve signup: joining means signing the Partner Program Addendum to the standard agreement. Rates and the minimum commitment are on the [partner page](https://siteqwality.com/partners/).

## The account model

```
Your partner account
├── Client account: Acme Corp        (monitors, status page, alerts, history)
├── Client account: Brightside Dental
└── Client account: Corner Cafe
```

One level, and only one. A client account cannot itself have client accounts.

| | Your partner account | A client account |
|---|---|---|
| Who signs in | Your team | Nobody, unless you invite them |
| Who is billed | You | Nobody. It has no payment method |
| Monitors, status pages, dashboards | Yours | Its own, entirely separate |
| Alert destinations | Yours | The client contact you set at creation |
| Plan | Your partner plan | An entitlement bundle you set |

A client account is an ordinary Site Qwality account except in two ways: it points at you as its partner, and its entitlements come from your bundle rather than from a card.

## What your clients see

| Surface | Whose brand |
|---|---|
| Status page | The client's, on the client's own domain, with no Site Qwality branding |
| Monthly uptime report | Yours |
| The dashboard | Ours |

Your clients rarely see the dashboard, because you are the one signing in. The status page's data hostname is visible in a browser's developer tools.

## Roles and access

Everything under `/partner` requires the **Admin** or **Super Admin** role on your own account. A Member, Viewer or Auditor on your account can work inside a client (if you send them there) but cannot create, re-plan or remove clients.

[API keys](/getting-started/account-and-team/#generate-api-keys) and [M2M clients](/enterprise/api-clients/) carry no role, so they resolve to Member and cannot use any `/partner` endpoint: manage clients from a signed-in session. An M2M client **can** work inside a client account with the `X-SQ-Account` header; an API key [cannot](/partners/context-switching/#which-credentials-may-switch).

## Billing

You are invoiced for the number of client accounts you hold, at the rate in your agreement, against a minimum commitment. Client accounts are never charged. If a client wants something outside their bundle, they can buy it retail on their own account, or you can talk to us.

## Your clients if you leave

Each client account gets a window, defined in the agreement, to convert into a direct retail account with us, keeping its data, monitors, status page and history.
