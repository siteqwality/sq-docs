SiteQwality's observability suite has five pillars:

- **Metrics** — numeric time-series with tags, dashboards, and alerting.
- **Logs** — structured and unstructured log search, with parsers and saved views.
- **Traces** — distributed tracing across your services.
- **RUM (Real User Monitoring)** — page-load and interaction telemetry from real users in the browser, with source-map support.
- **Session Replay** — pixel-perfect playback of user sessions for debugging UX issues.

All five pillars share the same query language, dashboards, and alerting fabric.
