## Send, verify, explore

The OTLP/HTTP origin is `https://otlp.siteqwality.com`, with `/v1/logs`, `/v1/traces` and `/v1/metrics`. JSON, remote-write and platform adapters have their own [routes and contracts](/observability/ingest/). A successful send means accepted records reached storage or durable spill. Confirm readable data separately; replay, processing, access and time filters can affect visibility.

Use stable `service.name`, `deployment.environment.name` and `service.version`. Resource identity joins related signals. Logs need actual trace/span IDs to link to a request. Metric exemplars link only to recorded trace IDs, and sampling or retention can make the target unavailable. [Correlation](/observability/traces/how-to/correlate-logs/) explains browser opt-in and CORS.

## Control data and cost

Logs and traces have independent decimal-GB allowances. Metrics use active hourly distinct series averaged over the month. Free allowances, paid packs, retention and hard-limit behavior are in [billing and retention](/observability/billing/). RUM, replay, uptime and incident plans keep their separate units.

Configure [processing pipelines](/observability/pipelines/) before expanding collection. Review [access, exports and erasure](/observability/privacy/) and keep ingest keys on servers. Telemetry read restrictions apply across explorers, saved views, exports and correlation.

**Telemetry alert notifications:** telemetry alert rules evaluate and show their state and history in the app, but they do not send notifications yet. Do not rely on a telemetry rule for paging. Uptime and incident notifications work as usual.
