Start with the [traces quickstart](/observability/traces/quickstart/) or select your [platform](/observability/platforms/overview/). Open [Traces](https://app.siteqwality.com/traces) and choose a recent time range, service and environment.

Search spans or trace summaries, then open the waterfall, flame graph and critical path. Inspect events, links, logs and resource attributes for the actual recorded request.

Results carry retention, partial-data and quality metadata, so a missing bucket or trace is not evidence of a healthy zero.
