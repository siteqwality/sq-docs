title: Control trace sampling
description: Choose SDK or Collector sampling while preserving useful context and honest estimates.
last_verified: 2026-10-18
owner: jj
---

Head sampling runs before export and reduces the telemetry received by Site Qwality. Configure a parent-based ratio sampler in your SDK, preserve the sampling decision across services and record the probability/threshold metadata the exporter supports. The backend cannot reconstruct individual requests that were never sent.

A Collector tail-sampling processor can retain errors and slow requests after seeing a trace. It needs enough memory, decision time and consistent routing for spans of the same trace. Tail sampling can bias latency/error estimates, so do not assume its retained subset represents all traffic without supported weights.

Customer processing pipelines can also sample or exclude received traces. Received-span RED derivation and trace retention are separate stages; inspect result `estimated`, partial and quality indicators. A graph built from incomplete spans may lack parents or dependencies. Sampling a trace away can leave an exemplar or RUM link without a retained target.

Use the [Collector guide](/observability/platforms/collector/) for transport and queues, then add the upstream [tail sampling processor](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector-contrib/tree/main/processor/tailsamplingprocessor) deliberately. Do not send production traffic through an unbounded queue. See [ingest limits](/observability/ingest/) and [trace reference](/observability/traces/reference/).
