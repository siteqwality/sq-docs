Anything else that speaks OTLP over HTTP works too: point it at `https://otlp.siteqwality.com` with your key in the `Authorization` header. See [Ingest routes and limits](/observability/ingest/).

## Check that it worked

Open [Traces](https://app.siteqwality.com/traces). Until your first span arrives, the page shows setup help, then it switches to your data on its own. Next, read the [traces reference](/observability/traces/reference/) or try a [query](/observability/query/).
