## Independent signal plans

Logs and traces each have their own pack, and one signal exhausting its allowance does not consume or stop another. A GB means **1,000,000,000 bytes**. Telemetry has no per-seat charge.

| Logs or traces, each | Monthly price | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| 5 GB | Free | 7 days |
| 50 GB | $15 | 30 days |
| 250 GB | $60 | 30 days |
| 1,000 GB | $200 | 30 days |

Billed bytes are accepted data before transport compression: the encoded OTLP protobuf for OTLP, the original UTF-8 record bytes for custom JSON and text, and the decoded records for platform payloads. Rejected records and data excluded by your rules do not count. Processed input, including excluded data, still has a separate abuse ceiling.

## Metric series

| Average active hourly series | Monthly price |
|---|---|
| 2,000 | Free |
| 10,000 | $25 |
| 50,000 | $100 |
| 200,000 | $350 |

An active series is a distinct metric identity with its resource and point label combination observed during an hour. Each hour's distinct count is averaged over the billing month. It is not a count of samples, events, unique label values or the largest daily peak, so sending a point every second instead of every minute adds no series. A user ID, request ID or changing URL in a label can create many series; keep those in logs or traces.

The Metrics cardinality view shows active series and expensive labels. Account and per-metric cardinality limits can reject **new series** while known series continue. Inspect metric partial-success reasons and Usage before increasing cardinality.

## Metric retention

| Representation | Free | Paid metrics pack |
|---|---|---|
| Raw points/distributions | 7 days | 15 days |
| Minute rollups | 45 days | 45 days |
| Hour rollups | 30 days | 395 days |

A rollup is an aggregate, not an archived raw event. Retention can remove raw detail, exact recomputation inputs and exemplar targets while a longer-lived chart remains.

## Limits and notifications

Usage emails are sent at **80%, 100% and 110%**. The 10% grace buffer ends at the hard 110% boundary, where the affected signal receives **429 with Retry-After**. Rate limits, processed-input ceilings and cardinality guards can refuse requests before a monthly allowance is exhausted. Queue data locally and honor retry guidance: refused data is not stored or billed.

There is no automatic overage billing and no separate spending cap. Telemetry packs cost the same per month on monthly or annual billing, with no annual discount. Buy paid packs in [Account billing](https://app.siteqwality.com/account?tab=billing). RUM, replay, monitors, status pages and incidents are priced separately.
