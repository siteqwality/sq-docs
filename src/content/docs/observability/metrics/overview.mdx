Start with the [metrics quickstart](/observability/metrics/quickstart/) or select your [platform](/observability/platforms/overview/). Open [Metrics](https://app.siteqwality.com/metrics) and choose a recent time range, service and environment.

Use the metric catalog to inspect types, units, labels and cardinality. Query raw samples or use the workspace for multi-query formulas, grouping, previous-period comparisons and distributions.

Results carry retention, partial-data and quality metadata, so a missing bucket or trace is not evidence of a healthy zero.
