Start with the [logs quickstart](/observability/logs/quickstart/) or select your [platform](/observability/platforms/overview/). Use the current explorer at [Site Qwality](https://app.siteqwality.com/logs) and choose a recent time range, service and environment.

Search text and typed fields, inspect event context and correlated traces, then use analytics, patterns or saved views.

Results carry retention, partial-data and quality metadata. Sampling, missing predecessors or unfinished derived work can affect interpretation. A missing bucket or retained trace is not evidence of a healthy zero.
