Anything else that speaks OTLP over HTTP works too: point it at `https://otlp.siteqwality.com` with your key in the `Authorization` header. See [Ingest routes and limits](/observability/ingest/).

## Check that it worked

Open [Logs](https://app.siteqwality.com/logs). Until your first record arrives, the page shows setup help, then it switches to your data on its own. Next, read the [logs reference](/observability/logs/reference/) or try a [query](/observability/query/).
