A schedule emails one [report](/reports/overview/) every day, week or month. The email carries the headline figures and short lists, and links to the full report as sent.

## Create a schedule

<Steps>

1. Open **Reports → Scheduled** and choose **Schedule a report**.
2. Pick the kind: executive summary, operations report or client uptime report. A client uptime report is monthly only.
3. Pick daily, weekly or monthly, the hour to send at and the timezone. The timezone defaults to the account's.
4. Add recipients: team members, outside addresses, or both.
5. Choose **Create schedule**. **Send test to me** on the schedule's row emails you the last complete period straight away.

</Steps>

On the Overview tab, **Schedule this report** opens the same form set to the report on screen.

## When it is sent

Each period goes out at the chosen hour once it has ended: a daily report the next day, a weekly report on Monday, and a monthly report on the 1st. It never goes out sooner than 20 minutes after the period ends, so a report set for midnight arrives at 00:20.

A client uptime report covers the UTC calendar month and is sent at the chosen hour, but no sooner than 4 hours after that month ends, once the month's last day of uptime data is in.

A new schedule sends its first report after the current period ends. Changing only a schedule's name or recipients keeps its next send time. If sending is held up, only the latest period goes out, never a backlog.

## Recipients

- **Team members** receive the report from the next send.
- **Outside addresses** get a confirmation email first, and nothing else is sent to them until they confirm. It names you and your account. The link expires after 7 days; resend it from the schedule. Adding outside addresses needs a verified email on your account.
- An address that bounced or marked an email as spam before gets no confirmation and no report. The Sent tab counts it as suppressed.

Every report email has an unsubscribe link, and mail apps can unsubscribe in one click. An unsubscribed recipient stays unsubscribed when the schedule is saved again.

- A **team member** who unsubscribes leaves that schedule only. To add them again, remove them, save, and add them back.
- An **outside address** that unsubscribes can't be added to any of your account's reports again until it resubscribes with the button on the unsubscribe page.

Outside addresses see on-call totals only, never the names of the people paged.

Reports from a client account you manage as a [partner](/partners/overview/) carry your brand, and come from your own domain once you verify it, like alert emails.

## Sent reports

**Reports → Sent** lists every period a schedule sent or tried to send, newest first, with how many emails went out. Open one to see the report exactly as it was sent.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sent` | Emails went out. A note says if some failed. |
| `failed` | The report could not be built or no email went out. It is tried again after 30 minutes, until the next period is due. |
| `skipped` | Nobody could receive it: no recipient has confirmed yet, or every address is suppressed. |

Sent reports are kept for 400 days. An email's link to the full report works for 90 days, and stops working once that address is removed or unsubscribes. Deleting a schedule deletes its sent reports.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Schedules per account | 20 |
| Recipients per schedule | 25 |
| Confirmation emails per account | 50 in any 24 hours |
| Confirmation emails to one address | 3 in any 24 hours, from all accounts |
| Resending one confirmation | Once a minute |
| Test sends | Once a minute per person, to that person only |

The same limits apply on every plan.

## API

Reading needs the `read:monitors` scope and changing schedules `write:monitors`. A key or token without `read:incidents` gets reports without incidents and on-call, and one without `read:rum` gets them without real user monitoring; the left-out sections are listed in `withheld`. Creating or changing an executive summary or operations report schedule needs both.

| Method and path | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /reports/subscriptions` | List schedules with their recipients. |
| `POST /reports/subscriptions` | Create a schedule. |
| `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /reports/subscriptions/{id}` | Read, replace or delete one. `PUT` replaces the whole schedule, recipients included. |
| `POST /reports/subscriptions/{id}/test` | Send the last complete period to the signed-in user. API keys are refused. |
| `POST /reports/subscriptions/{id}/recipients/{recipient_id}/resend` | Resend a pending confirmation. |
| `GET /reports/runs` | Sent reports, newest first. `subscription_id`, `limit` (1 to 100, default 50) and `before` (an RFC 3339 time, for the next page). |
| `GET /reports/runs/{id}` | One sent report, with the report as JSON in `snapshot` (`null` for a client uptime report). |
| `GET /reports/runs/{id}/document` | One sent report as a printable HTML page. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.siteqwality.com/reports/subscriptions" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SITEQWALITY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Weekly leadership summary",
    "kind": "executive",
    "frequency": "weekly",
    "send_hour": 8,
    "recipients": [{ "email": "cto@example.com" }]
  }'
```

`kind` is `executive`, `operations` or `client_uptime`, and `frequency` is `daily`, `weekly` or `monthly`. A team member is `{ "user_id": "..." }`. A 21st schedule gets `409`, and going over the confirmation or test limits gets `429`.
