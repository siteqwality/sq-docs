Sign up, then add your first monitor. Alerts work from the start: every new account has a notification group named **Default** that emails your sign-up address.

## 1. Create an account

<Steps>

1. Go to [app.siteqwality.com/auth/signup](https://app.siteqwality.com/auth/signup).

2. Sign up with an email link, Google, GitHub, or email and password. Email sign-up sends a link and a six-digit code: open the link or enter the code on the sign-up screen. No credit card is required.

</Steps>

## 2. Add your first monitor

Go to **Observe → Monitors**, open **Add Monitor** and choose **HTTP Monitor**. Enter a name and the URL to check, leave the rest as it is (a check every 5 minutes, alerts to the Default group) and click **Submit**.

To create it through the API, or to force a failure and watch the alert arrive, follow the [HTTP check quickstart](/uptime-monitoring/http-checks/quickstart/).

## How alerts reach you

- When the monitor fails, the Default group waits 3 minutes and emails you if it is still down. A failure that clears within those 3 minutes sends nothing.
- After a down email, you get an all-clear email once the monitor passes 2 checks in a row.
- No incident opens: monitor alerts go straight to notification groups. An incident opens when the monitor is linked to a [service](/incident-management/services/overview/) that creates incidents, or when you open one by hand.

To change who gets alerts, see [who gets notified](/incident-management/notifications/default-routing/). To page an on-call rotation and publish incidents on a status page, see [Incident Management](/incident-management/overview/).
