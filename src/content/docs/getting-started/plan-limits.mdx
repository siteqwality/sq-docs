Every product has a free tier and is priced on its own; prices are on the [pricing page](https://siteqwality.com/pricing/). Some limits lift once **any** product is on a paid plan, some need a paid plan on a specific product, and a few need Enterprise or a status page extra. Add-ons and status page extras on their own do not count as a paid plan.

## Monitors and checks

| Limit | Free | Paid plan |
|---|---|---|
| Monitors (HTTP, TCP port, ping, cron and browser combined, paused included) | 100 | Unlimited |
| Active cron monitors, within those 100 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Browser checks, within those 100 | 100 | Unlimited |
| [DNS record monitors](/uptime-monitoring/dns-record-monitors/overview/) | 3 | Unlimited |
| DNS record check interval | 1 hour minimum | 5 minutes minimum |

These limits follow one product's plan, not any paid plan: the check interval, HTTP, TCP port and ping check locations and rechecks from another cloud follow the **uptime** plan, and browser check locations the **Synthetic** plan.

| Limit | Free | Paid | Unlocked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uptime check interval | 5 minutes minimum | 30 seconds minimum | A paid uptime plan |
| Check locations per HTTP, TCP port or ping monitor | 1 | Any number | A paid uptime plan |
| [Recheck before alerting](/uptime-monitoring/http-checks/how-to/confirmation-threshold/#3-recheck-before-alerting) | Same location | Same location or another cloud | A paid uptime plan |
| Browser check locations per check | 1 | Any number | A paid Synthetic plan |

## Team

| Limit | Free | Paid plan | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seats | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| SAML single sign-on | No | No | Yes |
| SCIM provisioning | No | No | Yes |

A seat is a member or a pending invite. An invite stops using its seat when it expires or is canceled. Without Enterprise, an existing SSO or SCIM connection stays readable and can be deleted, but not changed, and members keep signing in through SSO until you delete it.

## On-call responders

The free plan includes 1 on-call responder, so rotations of more than one person need a responder pack. A responder is a distinct person in a rotation layer that has not ended, or named directly on an escalation level. Someone in three rotations and two levels is one responder. Overrides do not count, and neither does a schedule named on a level. The limit is checked when you add a layer or a level.

## Status pages

Every account includes one status page with full branding, and removing the "Powered by Site Qwality" badge is free. Buy more on **Settings → Billing → Status page extras**:

| Extra | Price | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| Additional status page | $10/mo ($100/yr) | 1 status page |
| Custom domain | $10/mo ($100/yr) | 1 custom domain |

Each unit of the retired White-Label extra still adds 1 status page and 1 custom domain. Extras share your plan's billing interval: added units are charged right away and apply once paid, and removed units end with the period you paid for.

## Other features

| Feature | Free | Unlocked by |
|---|---|---|
| Custom dashboards | No | A paid plan |
| SMS credit top-ups | No | A paid plan |
| Stored response body of a failed check | 5 days | 30 days with a paid plan, up to 365 on request |

Session replay and source map uploads work on every plan. Monthly usage allowances (checks, browser minutes, RUM sessions, replays, SMS credits) for each tier are on the [pricing page](https://siteqwality.com/pricing/); logs, metrics and traces are on [telemetry billing](/observability/billing/).

Limits agreed for your account replace the ones on this page.

## At a limit

Reaching a limit removes nothing you already have. Whatever would go over it is refused (a new monitor, invite, status page or domain, or a shorter check interval) until you are under the limit or upgrade. Usage-based products never bill overage: once the allowance and any grace buffer are used, they stop until the next period or an upgrade. For uptime checks, the dashboard won't save a monitor, or a change to one, that would take your monitors' projected monthly checks past the allowance, and offers fewer locations, a longer interval or an upgrade instead. Account settings show your usage.

Through the API, a request over a limit gets `402 Payment Required`, except check intervals, check locations, rechecks from another cloud and the browser check count, which get `403 Forbidden`.

## When a plan ends

A paid plan ends when you cancel and the period runs out, when you downgrade a product to free, or when a payment stays unpaid. Monitors keep running, and what the plan unlocked falls back to what your remaining plans allow:

- Without a paid uptime plan, checks that ran more often than every 5 minutes move to every 5 minutes. Without any paid plan, DNS record checks move to every hour.
- A monitor with more check locations than your plans allow keeps its first one.
- A monitor set to recheck from another cloud rechecks from the same location instead, and keeps the setting.
- Monitors above a limit keep running, but you can't add new ones or resume paused ones until you are under it.
- Stored response bodies older than the free window stop being readable. The window is a read window, so upgrading again makes them readable straight away.
- Custom dashboards stay, and you can still list, open, export and delete them. Creating, editing, importing, cloning and querying wait for a paid plan. Share links show "not found" until then, but you can still pause or revoke them.
- A new usage period starts that day with the free allowance. If your monitors would use it up before the month ends, the email says when they stop and when they start again.

Upgrade any time and the settings Site Qwality changed come back, except on monitors you edited since.

## When a payment fails

Stripe retries a failed payment. Before the last try, Site Qwality emails the date of the next attempt; after a hard decline, it asks for a new card. Billing emails go to every Super Admin and Billing Admin.

If the last try fails, the subscription is **unpaid** and the plan ends as above. The Billing tab offers two ways back and shows the amount before you confirm:

- **Restart** starts a new billing period today on your default card. Once it is paid, the plan comes back and the older unpaid invoices are voided.
- **Choose a different plan** ends the unpaid subscription and starts a new one.

After 30 days unpaid, Site Qwality cancels the subscription and voids its outstanding invoices. The account stays on the free plan.
