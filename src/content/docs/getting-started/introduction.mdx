Site Qwality combines uptime monitoring, observability (metrics, logs, traces and RUM), on-call paging and public status pages in one product, with a free tier and usage-based pricing for each.

## What's in the platform

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Uptime monitoring" icon="approve-check">
    HTTP, SSL/TLS, DNS, cron heartbeats, and full-browser synthetic checks, run on a schedule as often as every 30 seconds on paid uptime plans. HTTP checks run from your choice of 24 locations on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, across 18 countries and territories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Observability" icon="bars">
    Metrics, logs, traces, RUM, and session replay, each with its own explorer in the same dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Incident response" icon="warning">
    Incidents, on-call rotations, escalation policies, and notifications across email, SMS, Slack, Teams, Discord, Telegram, and webhooks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public status pages" icon="rocket">
    Branded, custom-domain status pages with components, scheduled maintenance, and email subscribers.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How the docs are organized

Each product area has the same shape:

- **Overview**: what the feature is, when to reach for it, and how it relates to the rest of the platform.
- **Quickstart**: the fastest copy-pasteable path to first success.
- **How-to guides**: task-shaped recipes ("rotate an on-call schedule," "add a custom domain to a status page").
- **Reference**: every field on every object, with defaults and constraints.

The [API Reference](/api-reference/) is generated from the API itself.

To work with Site Qwality from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another AI assistant, see [Connect AI assistants (MCP)](/integrations/mcp/).
